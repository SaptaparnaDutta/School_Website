About Our School

A Legacy of Learning, Values & Excellence

Swami Vivekananda Centenary School was established with the vision of providing high-quality English-medium education rooted in the philosophical ideals of Swami Vivekananda.

Located in Chakdaha, Nadia district of West Bengal, our school offers education from Play Group through Class XII under the CISCE Board (ICSE & ISC). We combine academic rigor with character development, producing students who are intellectually strong, morally grounded, and socially responsible.

Our educational approach emphasizes conceptual clarity, practical learning, and the all-round development of every child — nurturing their intellectual, emotional, physical, and creative faculties.

Academic Programs

Comprehensive Education for Every Stage

From early childhood learning through senior secondary, our structured curriculum builds strong academic foundations and essential life skills.

ICSE & ISC Curriculum

Affiliated with CISCE, our syllabus framework follows nationally recognized academic standards with comprehensive subject coverage.

Smart Classroom Learning

Interactive projectors, digital content, and modern teaching aids make lessons engaging and improve conceptual understanding.

Practical & Lab Education

Fully equipped science, computer, and mathematics labs provide hands-on experience with real-world experiments.

Co-Curricular Excellence

Debate, quiz, public speaking, art, music, dance, drama — developing confidence, creativity, and leadership.

Individual Student Support
